      2. Core skills: Building your first AI chatbot

      • 이 글은 CloudNet@ 팀 gasida님의 스터디 local ai on mac 내용 및 실습으로 작성된 글입니다.

       

      로컬 AI 스터디 2주차 — 핵심 기술: 첫 AI 챗봇 만들기

      • Part 2에서는 Ollama와 Python API를 활용해 로컬 LLM 기반 AI 챗봇을 직접 개발한다.

      • VS Code 및 Python 개발 환경을 구축하고, Streamlit을 활용한 웹 챗봇과 MLX Whisper 기반 음성 인식 기능을 구현한다.

      • 최종적으로 음성 입력, 대화 기록 및 컨텍스트 기억 기능을 갖춘 브라우저 기반 AI 챗봇을 완성한다.

However, since you have requested precision, I will define it with the exactness demanded in mathematical rigor, using the universally accepted chemical equation and precise terminology.

---

### Precise Definition:
Photosynthesis is the **biochemical process** by which photoautotrophic organisms (e.g., plants, algae, cyanobacteria) convert light energy into chemical energy, using **carbon dioxide (CO₂)** and **water (H₂O)** as reactants to produce **glucose (C₆H₁₂O₆)** and **oxygen (O₂)**, with **chlorophyll** as the essential light-absorbing pigment.

---

### Exact Chemical Equation (Balanced and Verified):
\[
\ce{6CO2 + 6H2O + \text{light energy} -> C6H12O6 + 6O2}
\]

#### Verification of Precision:
1. **Atom Balance (Mathematically Verified):**
   - Left side: 6 C, 12 O (from CO₂) + 12 H, 6 O (from H₂O) → **6 C, 12 H, 18 O**
   - Right side: 6 C, 12 H, 6 O (glucose) + 12 O (O₂) → **6 C, 12 H, 18 O**
   *The equation is stoichiometrically balanced.*

2. **Energy Input:**
   Light energy (typically 686 kcal/mol or 2870 kJ/mol) is required to drive the endergonic reaction. This is **not** a mathematical expression but a precisely measured thermodynamic value.

3. **Catalyst:**
   Chlorophyll *a* (C₅₅H₇₂MgN₄O₅) is the primary pigment, but the process requires a full photosynthetic apparatus (PSII, PSI, electron transport chain). **No mathematical model replaces this biological machinery.**

---

### Why This Is Not a Mathematical Concept:
- **No closed-form equation** describes the *entire process* (e.g., no single formula predicts photosynthetic rate under all conditions).
- **Variables** (light intensity, CO₂ concentration, temperature) follow empirical models (e.g., *Michaelis-Menten kinetics*), but these are **not mathematical definitions**—they are *applied* to describe the process.
- **Misuse of terminology:** "Photosynthesis" is **not** a function, set, or algebraic structure. It is a *biological phenomenon*.

---

### Final Statement (Mathematical Precision):
> **Photosynthesis** is a **biochemical reaction** with the **exact stoichiometric equation**
> \[
> \ce{6CO2 + 6H2O -> C6H12O6 + 6O2}
> \]
> driven by **light energy**, catalyzed by **chlorophyll-containing complexes**, and **not** a subject of mathematical analysis. Any attempt to reduce it to a mathematical object is a category error.
